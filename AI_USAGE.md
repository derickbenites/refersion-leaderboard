## What to write

- **Harness** — the editor, agent, or CLI that ran the model.
- **Model** — the model name that harness reported.
- **Prompt** — what was asked, in a few sentences. Do not paste hidden system instructions.
- **Outcome** — the files or decisions that came out of that session.
